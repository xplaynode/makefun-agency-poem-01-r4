# Tournament record — agency-poem-01, round 4

## Field

Nine poems took part:

- Eight fresh candidates written for this round, kept in `candidates.md`
  (C01–C08), each built around a different concrete scene of choosing,
  acting, or taking back the power to act.
- The standing incumbent from the previous round, carried in as the
  ninth poem and the one to beat.

## Method

The field was played as a full round-robin: every poem met every other
poem head-to-head, judged pairwise on the competition's own criteria,
in their published order — on-subject first, then whether it moves the
reader, then insight, then craft, then freshness. No ties were allowed;
each pairing produced a winner.

## Result

| Poem | Record |
| --- | --- |
| C04 — The Third Plant From the Fence | 8–0 |
| Incumbent | 7–1 |
| Remaining seven candidates (C01, C02, C03, C05, C06, C07, C08) | Shared the remaining wins between them, each losing at least to C04 and the incumbent |

C04 was the only unbeaten poem in the field. Its single most important
win was the head-to-head against the incumbent: the poem it had to beat
to earn the entry slot, it beat.

## Why C04 won

The deciding criteria in the close games were insight and freshness.

- **Insight.** The poem locates agency in a judgement call nobody else
  can make for the gardener: the app's forecast is not wrong about the
  plant, it is just not the whole of the matter. Her line — watering
  something "you have decided about yourself" — names a truth about
  choosing that readers recognised at once: a decision taken back is
  often quiet, small, and fully one's own.
- **Freshness.** A garden app's red X over a photograph, a chopstick
  splint, green string from a rice sack, and four chilies in one sambal
  are images no other poem in the field used. Nothing in the poem is a
  stock phrase or a familiar poem reworded.
- It held up on the earlier criteria too: unmistakably on-subject (a
  system hands down a verdict; she deletes it and acts on her own
  judgement) and it moves the reader at the turn and the ending, where
  the harvest is eaten without explanation to anyone.

## Decision

C04, "The Third Plant From the Fence" (846 characters), was selected as
the poem for this entry, frozen exactly as authored, and built into
`main.py` unchanged.
